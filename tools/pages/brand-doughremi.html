The everyday standard

Good enough for a Tuesday

Most bakeries save their best work for celebrations. DoughReMi exists because the croissant you grab on a Tuesday morning deserves the same lamination discipline as a wedding cake.

We bake in small batches from 6am so the counter is never stocked with something that came out four hours ago. Bread is baked on stone. Cream is dairy, never vegetable fat. Cakes are cut to order, and the message is piped by hand for free.

  • Three-day laminated viennoiserie, baked twice daily
  • 36-hour levain sourdough, three ingredients only
  • Real dairy cream and cream cheese — no substitutes
  • Every cake available eggless at no extra charge
