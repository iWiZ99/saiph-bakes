Frozen par-bake

Our croissant, your oven, twenty minutes

The hardest thing about wholesale viennoiserie is consistency across sites. Par-bake solves it: we do the three days of lamination and the first bake, you finish it fresh in front of your guests.

Product arrives blast-frozen at −18°C in cases of 40. From freezer to service is twenty minutes in a deck or convection oven — no proofing, no skill dependency, no 4am baker. Yield loss runs under 2%, and what you do not bake keeps for four months.
