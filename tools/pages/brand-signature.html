The philosophy

The Art of Fine Desserts

Where precision, imagination and exceptional ingredients come together.

A Signature entremet is not a cake with decoration on top. It is nine deliberate components — biscuit, croustillant, insert, crémeux, mousse, glaze, chantilly, garnish and finish — each set at its own temperature, assembled in reverse, frozen, unmoulded and glazed in a five-minute window that does not forgive hesitation.

Nothing is scaled up from a home recipe. Everything is built for the way sugar, fat and water actually behave.

  • Single-origin couverture from Ghana, Madagascar and Vietnam
  • Kashmiri saffron, Sicilian pistachio, Piedmont IGP hazelnut
  • Second-flush Assam tea from our own partner estates
  • Every piece photographed and chef-signed before dispatch

Inside the couture kitchen

Five rooms, five temperatures

{{ICON_THERMOMETER}}

Chocolate room · 18°C

Tempering, shell casting and airbrushing. Humidity held under 50% so bloom never forms.

{{ICON_LAYERS}}

Assembly · 4°C

Inserts frozen to −18°C, then built in reverse inside silicone moulds. Nine layers, two millimetre tolerance.

{{ICON_SPARKLES}}

Finishing · by hand

Velvet spray, gold leaf, tempered chocolate sails and sugar florals. No moulds for garnish work.

{{ICON_CHECKCIRCLE}}

Sign-off · every piece

Photographed, weighed, and released only when a pastry chef puts their initials on the docket.
