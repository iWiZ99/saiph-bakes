About SAiPH Bakes

One kitchen. Three brands. One standard.

SAiPH Bakes is a modern bakery and food company from Dibrugarh, built on craftsmanship, quality, innovation and hospitality — and on the belief that a Tuesday croissant deserves the same discipline as a wedding cake.

Founded 2019 7 stores 3 consumer brands 140+ people 8 delivery cities
Our story

It started because nothing in the city was quite right

In 2019 you could get a good cake in Dibrugarh or a good croissant in Dibrugarh, but rarely both from the same place, and almost never on a weekday morning.

We started with one deck oven, a levain jar and a rule we still keep: nothing goes on the counter that a chef would not eat themselves. The first year was bread and croissants. The second added celebration cakes because customers kept asking. The third brought a chocolate room, because the desserts we wanted to make needed 18°C and a dehumidifier, not good intentions.

By 2023 it was obvious that one brand could not hold all of it honestly. A ₹145 croissant and an ₹18,500 wedding tier do not belong under the same sign — they need different service, different pricing, different rooms. So we split into three consumer brands under one parent house, sharing a single production standard.

  • DoughReMi — the everyday counter, baked through the day
  • SAiPH Signature — the couture kitchen, finished by hand
  • Khowdy — the Assam pantry, made clean and shelf-stable

Sourcing

If we cannot source it well, we do not make it

There is no version of this business where we save four rupees on butter. The ingredient list is the recipe.

01

Cultured French butter, 82% fat

Lamination is a fat-crystal problem. Table butter cannot do it and we stopped pretending otherwise in month four.

02

Single-origin couverture

Ghana 70%, Madagascar 64%, Vietnam 73% — bought as beans-to-bar couverture, never compound.

03

Fourteen partner tea gardens

Second-flush orthodox leaf from Jorhat, bought direct at a premium and packed within ten days of plucking.

04

No palm oil, anywhere

Cold-pressed coconut and mustard oil in the Khowdy range. It costs more and lasts about as long.
