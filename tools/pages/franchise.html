The opportunity

A premium bakery brand at the start of its national run

India's organised bakery market is growing at double digits, and the Northeast and East are chronically under-served at the premium end. We have proved the model in Dibrugarh and are looking for the first partners to take it further.

What we are looking for is not passive investors. It is operators who will stand at the counter in the first year, who care that the croissant is served before ten, and who want a brand with a real production standard behind it rather than a franchise-in-a-box.

  • Central production means your store needs a finishing kitchen, not a bakery
  • Three brands under one roof lets a single site serve morning, gifting and celebration demand
  • Our kitchen supplies Dibrugarh today; partner territories open as the cold chain extends
  • Territory rights granted on a first-qualified basis, not first-enquired

The process

From enquiry to opening in twelve weeks

Week 1–2

Application & discovery call

Territory availability confirmed, format shortlisted, and the financial model shared under NDA.

Week 3–4

Site survey & approval

Footfall study, catchment mapping and a go/no-go on your shortlisted properties. We say no often.

Week 5–6

Agreement & design

Five-year agreement signed, turnkey design kit issued, equipment ordered and fit-out contractor briefed.

Week 7–10

Fit-out & training

Four weeks at the Dibrugarh craft bakery for you and your head of counter, while the site is built.

Week 11–12

Soft launch & opening

Dry runs, local launch campaign, and our operations team on site for the first ten trading days.
