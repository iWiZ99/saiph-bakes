The brownie argument

No cocoa powder shortcuts. Melted 54% Belgian couverture, brown butter, and just enough flour to hold it together, baked to a crackled top with a deliberately underbaked centre and toasted walnuts through it. Warm it for eleven seconds before you eat it. We will not be taking questions.

  • Belgian couverture, never compound chocolate
  • Real dairy cream in every pastry — no vegetable fat
  • Tarts blind-baked to a snap and torched to order
  • Cupcake boxes of six or twelve, up to three flavours

Party-ready cupcake boxes

Six or twelve cupcakes in up to three flavours, piped with Swiss meringue buttercream and finished with sprinkles, edible glitter or custom acrylic toppers. Add a personalised message card at checkout. Available eggless.

From the Signature counter

If the occasion warrants it, the pâtisserie case has passionfruit mango tarts with torched Italian meringue and a hazelnut Paris-Brest piped with Piedmont praline mousseline we roast and grind ourselves.
